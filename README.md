# Bound SMP

## 1. Points System
* **Points:** This is the currency that is kept on your player. When you die to a **player**, you lose points. They are also what you spend to obtain a Sanction Orb.
* **Tokens:** The physical version of points. When you visit the altar at spawn, you can convert points to physical tokens. This lets you store them in your ender chest to avoid losing them in a fight, or if you want to give them to a friend.

### Token Crafting Recipe
* **Ingredients:** 4 Netherite Scraps + 4 Echo Shards + 1 Diamond Block

---

## 2. Mechanics
* **Player Kills:** Killing another player gives you +1 points.
* **Death Penalty:** If you are killed by a player, you lose -1 points. Dying to anything else does not cause you to lose points.
* **Ban Reset:** If you kill a player who has banned items, those items are automatically unbanned.
* **The Altar:** At spawn, there is an altar where you can submit points in order to buy Sanction Orbs.
* **Starting Points:** Everyone starts with exactly 1 point.

---

## 3. Ban Process

* **Price:** An orb to ban the common tier of items is 5 points, while the orb to ban the rare tier is 15 points.
* **RNG:** When you use an orb, it gives you 3 choices of items to ban from whichever tier you bought, and you can reroll these 3 items a total of two times.
* **Anonimity:** When you ban an item, the server broadcasts the item that is banned. However, it does not say who banned it.
* **Restrictions:** The banned item is not removed from anyone's inventory, however you cannot use it in any way.

## 4. Custom Weapons

### Altar's Edge
* **Acquisition:** Obtained during a server-wide event.
* **Ability:** When you kill someone, there is a 75% chance to gain triple the points. The dead player still only loses 1 point however.
* **Risk:** If a player dies with it in their inventory, they are guaranteed to lose 20% of their total points.

### Greed Blade
* **Acquisition:** Obtained during a server-wide event.
* **Ability:** Deals +0.5 more attack damage for every 2 points the player has.
* **Risk:** Players are required to not hoard points in their ender chest, so when they die, they may lose many points.
